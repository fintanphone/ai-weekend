# Expenses Policy

Expenses must be submitted within 60 days of being incurred. Submissions after
that window require director approval and are usually refused.

Mileage is reimbursed at the current Revenue civil service rate. Employees
should not claim mileage for travel between home and their normal place of work.

Meals are reimbursable up to 35 euro per day when travelling overnight on
company business. Alcohol is not reimbursable under any circumstances.

Any single item over 250 euro requires pre-approval from a line manager before
purchase, not after.
