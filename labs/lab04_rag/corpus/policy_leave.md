# Leave Policy

Aurora Freight employees accrue 25 days of annual leave per year, accruing
monthly from the start date. Leave does not carry over into the following
calendar year except by written exception from a director.

Employees in the Cork warehouse operate on a shift pattern and accrue leave in
hours rather than days, at a rate equivalent to 25 days for a full-time
contract.

Public holidays are additional to the 25 days and are not deducted from the
allowance.

Requests for more than five consecutive days must be submitted at least three
weeks in advance through the Workday system.
