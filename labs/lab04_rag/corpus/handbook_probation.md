# Probation

New employees serve a six-month probation period. During probation, notice
period is two weeks on either side.

After probation, the standard notice period is one month for all staff below
manager grade, and three months for manager grade and above.

Annual leave accrues during probation but may not be taken in the first two
months of employment except in exceptional circumstances.

Probation may be extended once, by up to three months, with written reasons.
