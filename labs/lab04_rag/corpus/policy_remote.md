# Remote Working

Office-based staff may work remotely up to two days per week. This does not
apply to warehouse or logistics floor roles, which are onsite by necessity.

Remote days must be agreed with your line manager and recorded in the team
calendar. There is no formal application process.

Staff working remotely from outside Ireland for more than 10 consecutive
working days must notify People Operations, as this can create tax residency
obligations for the company.

Equipment for home working is provided on request up to a value of 400 euro.
